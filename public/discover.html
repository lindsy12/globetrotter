About GlobeTrotter

GlobeTrotter began as a simple idea: what if exploring Yaoundé felt less like scrolling through a directory, and more like getting a recommendation from someone who actually knows the city?

We're building a travel companion rooted in this place — surfacing destinations genuinely worth your time, with an honest eye toward who benefits from your visit and how the city is treated in return. Every recommendation reflects real neighborhoods and real local knowledge, not generic listings.

GlobeTrotter is a growing project, built one honest feature at a time.
